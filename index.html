Our Story

More Than
Just Things

At Ole Vintage Finds, we believe the best treasures are the ones that carry history. Every item in our 9,200+ square foot showroom has been carefully chosen for its character, craft, and the quiet stories it holds.

“Every piece has a story — we’re just helping it find the next chapter.”
  • Over 9,200 sq ft of climate-controlled browsing space
  • More than 50 unique vendors under one roof
  • Antiques, handmade crafts & one-of-a-kind home décor
  • Friendly staff, great prices — always a new find
